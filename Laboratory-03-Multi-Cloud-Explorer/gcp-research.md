Google Cloud Platform Research

Brief Overview

Google Cloud Platform is Google's cloud computing services, with roots dating back to google app engine in 2008.GCP is known for its strengths in data analytics, artificial intelligence, machine learning, and container orchestration, since Google itself created the kubernetes technology used widely across the industry.

Global Infrastructure

GCP infrastructure is divided into "Regions" and "Zones". A distinguishing feature of GCP is its use of Google's own private global fiber-optic  network, which connect its data centers and is also used to power services like Google Search and YouTube, resulting in fast and reliable network performance.

Cloud Management Console

The Google Cloud Console is the web-based interface for managing GCP resources. It has a clean, simple design consistent with other Google products, making it approachable for users already familiar with Google's ecosystem.
